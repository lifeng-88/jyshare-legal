Privacy Policy

Drive & Glam · Effective August 20, 2026

Drive & Glam ("the App") is an automotive photo gallery for iPhone. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App handles and how it is stored.

Summary

  • No account or sign-in required
  • No data is sent to our servers
  • All photos and app data stay on your device
  • No third-party analytics or advertising

Data Stored on Your Device

The App may store the following locally on your iPhone:

  • Photos you upload through the App
  • Favorites, browse history, and search history
  • Display preferences (language, theme, sort/filter settings)
  • A locally generated device identifier used for app functionality
  • Temporary image cache to improve performance

This data is not transmitted to us or any third party.

Photo Library & Camera Access

With your permission, the App can access your photo library to let you select images for upload, and save images to your library when you choose Save or Wallpaper. The App may also access your camera if you choose to take a new photo. These permissions are used only for features you initiate. We do not access your photos in the background.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Name, email, or contact information (unless you email us for support)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to external services
  • Advertising identifiers

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Deletion

You can delete uploaded photos and clear cache at any time from Me → Settings. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored app data from your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised accordingly.
