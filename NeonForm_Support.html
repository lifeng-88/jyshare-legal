Quick start

  • Open Library to browse exercises, bookmark favorites, or add moves to today’s plan.
  • On Training, start today’s plan or a recommended program. Use pause, skip, and rest controls as needed.
  • Check Analytics for weekly trends, muscle load, and history (long-press a row to delete).
  • In Profile, set goals, rest time, voice coaching, and Language (System / English / 简体中文).

FAQ

How do I switch language inside the App?

Go to Profile → Preferences → Language. Choose System, English, or 简体中文. The change applies immediately and is saved on device.

Where is my workout data stored?

History, bookmarks, plans, and profile settings are stored locally on your iPhone. Uninstalling the App removes that sandbox data.

The workout paused after I left the App

This is intentional for safety and accuracy. Resume from the Training tab when you return. If the App was force-quit mid-session, NeonForm tries to restore the unfinished workout on next launch.

Voice coaching is silent

Enable “Live voice coaching” in Profile, raise media volume, and ensure Silent Mode / Focus settings allow playback. Spoken cues use the system speech engine for the active app language.

How do I reset demo / local data?

Open Profile and use “Clear local data”. This wipes history and preferences stored by NeonForm and reseeds demo history. Your in-app language preference is kept.

Is NeonForm medical advice?

No. NeonForm is a fitness aid. Stop if you feel pain and consult a professional when needed. See the User Agreement for full terms.

常见问题

如何在应用内切换语言？

进入个人资料 → 训练偏好 → 语言，选择「系统」「English」或「简体中文」。切换立即生效，并保存在本机。

训练数据保存在哪里？

历史记录、收藏、计划与个人设置保存在 iPhone 本地。卸载应用会清除对应沙盒数据。

离开应用后训练被暂停了

这是出于安全与准确性的设计。返回后可在训练页继续。若训练中途被强杀，下次启动时 NeonForm 会尝试恢复未完成的训练。

语音纠偏没有声音

请在个人资料开启「实时语音纠偏」，调高媒体音量，并检查静音/专注模式。播报使用系统语音引擎，语言跟随应用内语言设置。

如何清除演示 / 本地数据？

打开个人资料，使用「清除本地数据」。这将清空 NeonForm 保存的历史与偏好，并重新写入演示数据。应用内语言偏好会保留。

NeonForm 算医疗建议吗？

不算。NeonForm 是健身辅助工具。如感不适请立即停止，必要时咨询专业人士。完整条款见用户协议。
