常见问题

手帐存在哪里？
存在这台 iPhone 的应用数据里，包括文字、心情、草稿、收藏和你插入的照片。开发者的服务器不会收到这些内容。
如何删除一篇手帐？
打开那一篇，按删除。删除后，这篇不再留在应用的本地记录中。
如何导出？
在「心屿」或「岁月」里使用导出手帐。系统会打开分享面板，你可以把它发给自己选定的应用或联系人。
照片会上传吗？
不会。相机和相册只在你主动插入图片时使用，照片保存在应用本地目录。
如何关闭提醒？
在「心屿」的晨昏提醒里关闭，或到 iPhone「设置 › 通知 › Chéri」中关闭通知。
卸载后数据还在吗？
卸载会去掉这台设备上的应用数据。如果之前做过设备备份，备份里可能仍有一份，需要在 Apple 的备份里处理。

Common questions

Where is my journal stored?
On this iPhone, in the app’s data. That includes text, moods, drafts, saved inspirations, and photos you add. The developer’s servers do not receive it.
How do I delete an entry?
Open the entry and delete it. It is then removed from the app’s local record.
How do I export?
Use export from Profile or Journey. The system share sheet opens, and you can send the text to an app or person you choose.
Are my photos uploaded?
No. The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to add a picture. The photo stays in the app’s local folder.
How do I turn reminders off?
Turn them off under reminders in Profile, or in iPhone Settings › Notifications › Chéri.
Does uninstalling keep my data?
Uninstalling removes the app’s data from that device. A device backup made earlier may still hold a copy, which has to be removed in Apple’s backup.
