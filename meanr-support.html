快速开始

  1. 从相册选择一张正脸照片，或使用前置相机拍摄
  2. 选择风格（推荐「漫画脸」或「动漫风」）
  3. 点击「一键生成」，在结果页调整强度或切换风格
  4. 保存至相册，或在「我的」页面管理作品

所有 AI 风格化均在设备本地完成，照片不会上传云端。

使用技巧

选择清晰正脸

光线均匀、无遮挡的正脸照片效果最佳。可从相册选取，或使用前置相机拍摄。

调整风格强度

强度较低时保留更多细节；强度较高时风格化效果更明显。可在结果页微调，无需重新生成。

按分类浏览风格

「AI 模型」提供漫画、动漫等风格；「艺术滤镜」提供油画、水彩、赛博朋克等效果。

快速切换风格

在结果页点击风格标签，可用同一张照片重新生成并对比效果。

管理我的作品

作品保存在「我的」页面。可查看详情、分享、保存至相册或删除。

常见问题

生成速度慢或卡顿？

AI 模型在设备端运行，首次使用某风格时可能需要加载模型。请关闭其他占用内存的应用，并在 iPhone 上保持充足存储空间。

无法访问相册或相机？

请前往 iOS「设置 → Meanr」，开启「照片」与「相机」权限。若已拒绝，需手动开启后重启应用。

无法保存到相册？

请确认已授予 Meanr「添加照片」权限。可在「设置 → Meanr → 照片」中选择「所有照片」或「添加照片」。

我的照片会被上传吗？

不会。核心 AI 风格化在您的 iPhone 本地完成，原始照片不会为 AI 处理上传至我们的服务器。详情请参阅隐私政策。

支持哪些设备？

Meanr 需要 iOS 15.0 或更高版本，仅支持 iPhone 竖屏模式。

Quick Start

  1. Select a front-facing photo from your library or capture one with the front camera
  2. Choose a style (we recommend Comic Face or Anime)
  3. Tap Generate, then adjust intensity or switch styles on the result screen
  4. Save to Photos or manage works from the Profile tab

All AI stylization runs on your device. Photos are never uploaded to the cloud.

Tips

Choose a Clear Front Face

Even lighting and an unobstructed front face work best. Pick from your album or use the front camera.

Adjust Style Intensity

Lower intensity keeps more detail; higher intensity applies more stylization. Fine-tune on the result screen without regenerating.

Browse by Category

AI Models for comic/anime looks; Art Filters for oil paint, watercolor, cyberpunk, and more.

Quick Style Switch

Tap style tags on the result screen to regenerate with the same photo and compare.

Manage My Works

Works are saved on Profile. View details, share, save to Photos, or delete.

FAQ

Slow generation or lag?

AI models run on-device. The first time you use a style, the model may need to load. Close memory-heavy apps and ensure your iPhone has enough free storage.

Can't access Photos or Camera?

Go to iOS Settings → Meanr and enable Photos and Camera permissions. If previously denied, you must enable them manually and restart the app.

Can't save to Photos?

Make sure Meanr has permission to add photos. In Settings → Meanr → Photos, choose "All Photos" or "Add Photos Only".

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Core AI stylization runs locally on your iPhone. Original photos are not uploaded to our servers for AI processing. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Which devices are supported?

Meanr requires iOS 15.0 or later and supports iPhone in portrait orientation only.
