FAQ

Is WildQuest fully offline?
Compass, emergency tools, survival guide, and waypoints work offline. Some article images may load when you are online.
How do I change language?
Profile → Language → System, English, or Simplified Chinese.
Where is my data stored?
On device only (UserDefaults & Keychain). See the Privacy Policy.
Do I need an account?
No account is required for core offline features.
Emergency tools disclaimer
SOS, whistle tone, and flashlight are aids only — not guaranteed emergency services.
