Luminous Muse ("we", "our", or "the App") is an AI portrait studio application for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

Summary: Luminous Muse is designed to process your photos on your device. We do not sell your personal data. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how you use the App, the following information may be stored on your device:

  • Photos you select or capture — portraits uploaded from your photo library or camera for style transformation.
  • Generated results — transformed images saved to your in-app history.
  • Profile information — display name, handle, and optional avatar you choose to set.
  • App preferences — settings such as theme (light/dark), language, style intensity, and whether to keep original photos.
  • Favorites and drafts — templates you favorite and in-progress drafts.
  • Device identifier (dev_id) — a randomly generated UUID stored in the iOS Keychain to support diagnostics and support requests. It is not used for advertising.
  • Notifications — in-app notification history generated locally by the App.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information above solely to:

  • Apply AI-style portrait transformations to your selected photos;
  • Display your creation history, favorites, and profile within the App;
  • Save images to your photo library when you explicitly choose to export;
  • Remember your preferences between sessions;
  • Respond to support inquiries when you voluntarily provide your dev_id.

3. Photo Library & Camera Access

The App requests access to your photo library so you can select portraits for transformation and optionally save results. Camera access may be requested if you choose to capture a new portrait. You can revoke these permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.

4. Data Storage & Retention

  • Most App data is stored locally on your iPhone in the App’s sandbox.
  • Original photos are stored only if you enable “Keep original photos” in Account Privacy settings.
  • You may delete all App data at any time via Profile → Account Privacy → Clear All Data.
  • Uninstalling the App removes locally stored data from your device, except items already saved to your photo library.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your photos or profile data with third parties for marketing purposes. Information is not transmitted to our servers unless you explicitly contact us and choose to share details for support.

6. Tracking & Analytics

Luminous Muse does not use App Tracking Transparency (ATT) frameworks for cross-app tracking. We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs. The App may access file timestamps for local file management as declared in our App Store privacy manifest.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will assist with deletion.

8. Your Rights & Choices

  • Control photo library and camera permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Toggle whether original photos are retained in App settings.
  • Clear all locally stored App data from within the App.
  • Export or delete images saved to your photo library using the Photos app.

9. Security

We use industry-standard iOS security features, including sandboxed storage and Keychain protection for the device identifier. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure; please keep your device passcode and biometric protections enabled.

10. International Users

The App is operated from the jurisdictions where it is made available on the App Store. By using the App, you understand that data is processed and stored on your local device.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, contact us at privacy@luminousmuse.app or visit our Support page.
