Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026 · WildQuest iOS App

Overview

WildQuest (“we”, “us”, “the App”) is an outdoor companion for compass-style telemetry, emergency utilities, survival reference, and waypoint logging. We design the App to work offline first. Most information you enter stays on your device. This Privacy Policy describes what is stored locally, when the App may use the network, and how you can control your data.

Information stored on your device

  • Profile & medical card fields — blood type, allergies, tetanus status, emergency contact (optional), edited by you in Profile.
  • Waypoints & expedition log — coordinates, notes, and timestamps you save.
  • Preferences — language, night mode, bookmarked survival articles.

Network use

Core features work offline. When you are online, the App may load article hero images, map tiles, or other third-party media. We do not upload your profile or waypoint content to WildQuest-operated servers as part of those features.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not require an account for core offline features.
  • We do not provide user-generated content feeds or social networking inside the App.

Third parties

Apple (App Store) and image or map hosts may process technical data (such as IP address and device type) under their own policies when you load online media.

Children

WildQuest is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Edit or clear profile fields, delete waypoints, and reset preferences in the App. Uninstalling WildQuest removes the App’s local data from your device.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions: WildQuest Support or support@wildquest.app.
