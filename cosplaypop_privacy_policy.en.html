Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Introduction

CosplayPop ("CosplayPop", "we", "us", or "our") is a cosplay community application that helps creators share works, discover content, and connect with others. Some features may be delivered through embedded web experiences inside the app.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, the choices available to you, and how to contact us. By downloading, accessing, or using CosplayPop, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use CosplayPop, we may collect or process the following categories of information:

  • Profile and account information you provide, such as display name, bio, avatar, cover image, and other profile fields.
  • User content you create or upload, including photos, captions, tags, published works, comments, messages, and related metadata.
  • Activity and preferences stored on your device or associated with your use of the app, such as likes, bookmarks, follows, notification settings, and language preferences.
  • Device and app information, including app version, platform, system version, locale, distribution channel, and a device identifier (dev_id) generated locally and stored in the iOS Keychain to support app functionality, configuration, diagnostics, and fraud prevention.
  • Attribution and analytics information, such as campaign/source data, install attribution payloads, and event names or parameters you or the app submit for analytics purposes.
  • Push notification data, including your choice to enable notifications, device token, delivery status, and notification payload content needed to open relevant in-app content.
  • Photos and media you choose from your photo library, camera, or downloads when editing your profile, publishing content, saving creations, or using embedded web features.
  • Cached and local files, including images, videos, JSON cache entries, and other files stored locally to improve performance and offline usability.
  • Support and communication data if you contact us, including the content of your message and contact details you provide.

How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • provide, operate, maintain, and improve CosplayPop and its features;
  • deliver profile, publishing, browsing, search, messaging, events, notifications, and related community functions;
  • determine app configuration, feature availability, and embedded web experiences;
  • register and deliver push notifications you request or authorize;
  • measure installs, attribution, product usage, and campaign performance;
  • cache media and settings locally to improve loading speed and reliability;
  • detect, investigate, and prevent abuse, fraud, security incidents, and violations of our Terms of Service;
  • comply with applicable law and respond to lawful requests; and
  • communicate with you about the app, policy updates, or support requests.

Permissions and Device Storage

CosplayPop may request access to your photo library so you can upload profile images, publish works, pick photos for embedded features, or save media to your album. You can manage photo permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

CosplayPop may request permission to send push notifications. If you decline, certain notification features may not work, but other app functions may still be available.

Much of your data is stored locally on your device, including profile data, preferences, cached media, and some configuration values. The dev_id is stored in the iOS Keychain. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored data that is not backed up elsewhere.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • With other users, according to the visibility settings you choose for your profile and published content.
  • For legal and safety reasons, including to comply with law, enforce our terms, respond to lawful requests, or protect the rights, safety, and integrity of users and CosplayPop.
  • In connection with a business transaction, such as a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

International Data Transfers

CosplayPop may be operated from or supported by systems located outside your country or region. Where required, we take steps designed to protect information transferred across borders in accordance with applicable law.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Locally stored data remains on your device until you edit, delete, or uninstall the app, subject to iOS system behavior and backups.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information, or to withdraw consent where processing is consent-based.

You can also:

  • edit or update profile information in the app;
  • manage photo library and notification permissions in iOS Settings;
  • change app language in Settings;
  • delete content you created, where the feature is available;
  • disable push notifications in iOS Settings; and
  • uninstall the app to remove local app data from your device.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Children’s Privacy

CosplayPop is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information. However, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy
