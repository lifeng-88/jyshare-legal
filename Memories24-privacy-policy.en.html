AlumniRecord

Privacy Policy

Effective: September 5, 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Introduction

AlumniRecord (“the App”) is a digital yearbook for organizing class memories, classmate profiles, and messages. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data the App handles, how it is stored, and your rights.

By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the App and delete it from your device.

2. Data We Collect

No account is required. We may collect the following data:

  • Content you create: memory photos, titles, captions, tags, classmate profiles (name, birthday, hometown, hobbies, quote, avatar), messages, and favorites.
  • App preferences: interface language (English / Traditional Chinese), notification and privacy toggles.
  • Device identifier (dev_id): an anonymous UUID stored in Keychain to help with support requests. You can copy it from the About screen when reporting issues.
  • Photos you select: when you pick images from your library, only the items you choose are read to create memories or avatars.

The App does not currently use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

3. How We Use Data

  • Display, edit, search, and manage your memories and classmate content inside the App.
  • Save favorites, messages, and related state for the next launch.
  • Save images to your photo library when you use Share or Save to Album (with your permission).
  • Help troubleshoot issues when you voluntarily provide your dev_id to support.

4. Retention

  • Deleting the App may remove related data; the Keychain dev_id may persist depending on system behavior.
  • Please keep copies of content that is important to you.

5. Permissions

  • Photos / Photo Library: to pick images and, with your consent, save memory images to your library.

You can manage permissions anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal content with third parties except:

  • When you use the iOS share sheet to send content to other apps or contacts.
  • When required by law or to protect rights and safety.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for class yearbook use. Users under the age of consent in their region should use the App with guardian approval. We do not knowingly collect more personal data from children than the App functionality requires.

8. Your Rights

Inside the App you can:

  • Edit or delete memories, classmates, and messages you created.
  • Clear favorites and search state by removing content or reinstalling the App.
  • Change language and related settings.

For other data requests, contact us through the Support page.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy for feature or legal changes. The effective date on this page will reflect the latest version. Material changes will be communicated through in-app notice or other reasonable means.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: support@memories24.app
