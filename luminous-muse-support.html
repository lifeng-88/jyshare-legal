Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a styled portrait?
Open Discover, choose a template, tap the portrait cell to select a photo from your library, then tap Generate. View the result in History or the result screen.
Why can't I access my photos?
Go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Luminous Muse and allow access. You may choose “Selected Photos” or “Full Access” depending on your preference.
How do I save a creation to my photo library?
On the result screen, tap Save. The App will request permission to add photos if not already granted.
Where is my data stored?
History, drafts, favorites, and profile settings are stored locally on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.
How do I delete all my App data?
Profile → Account Privacy → Clear All Data. This removes history, drafts, favorites, and resets your profile. Images already saved to your photo library are not deleted.
Can I keep my original photos?
Yes. Enable “Keep original photos” in Profile → Account Privacy. When disabled, only the transformed result is stored in history.
Does the App work offline?
Yes. Style processing runs on your device. An internet connection is not required for core transformation features.
How do I switch language or theme?
Profile → Settings & Preferences → Language or App Theme (light/dark toggle).

Troubleshooting

App feels slow or stuck

  • Force quit and reopen the App.
  • Ensure sufficient free storage on your device.
  • Restart your iPhone if problems persist.

Generation failed

  • Try a different portrait with clear facial visibility.
  • Use a photo with adequate resolution (not extremely small or blurry).
  • Clear drafts or old history if storage is low.

Save to Photos failed

  • Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Luminous Muse → Add Photos Only or Full Access.
  • Verify available storage on your device.
