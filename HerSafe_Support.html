联系支持

产品咨询、隐私请求、功能反馈或漏洞报告，请发送邮件：

support@hersafe.app

请在邮件中注明系统版本、机型与问题复现步骤，便于我们更快协助。

通常 1–3 个工作日内回复

常见问题

  • SOS 无反应？请检查是否授予必要权限，并确认未处于静音限制场景。
  • 定位不显示？请在系统设置中允许定位，并在开阔环境重试。
  • 紧急联系人为空？正式版不会预置个人数据，请在「应急配置」中自行添加。
  • 语言如何切换？应用内「关于」页可切换中文 / English / 跟随系统。

安全提示

  • 真实危险请优先拨打当地紧急电话。
  • 伪装来电与警报为辅助工具，请合法合规使用。
  • 请定期确认紧急联系人号码仍然有效。

Contact Support

For product questions, privacy requests, feedback, or vulnerability reports, email:

support@hersafe.app

Include OS version, device model, and steps to reproduce so we can help faster.

Typical reply within 1–3 business days

FAQ

  • SOS not responding? Check required permissions and silent-mode limits.
  • No location? Allow Location in Settings and retry outdoors.
  • Empty contacts? Release builds do not seed personal demo data—add contacts in Config.
  • Language? Switch Chinese / English / System in the in-app About page.

Safety Notes

  • In real danger, call local emergency services first.
  • Disguise calls and alarms are assistive tools—use them lawfully.
  • Periodically verify that emergency contact numbers are still valid.
