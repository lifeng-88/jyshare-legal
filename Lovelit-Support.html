FAQ

Where is my data stored? Notes, moments, photos, and dates stay on this device and are not uploaded to our servers. Back up your iPhone before uninstalling or clearing cache.
Reminders are not arriving. Enable notifications for Lovelit in iPhone Settings → Notifications, then turn the reminder on in the app. Local reminders require system permission.
I cannot add a photo. Allow Lovelit to access Photos in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. Images are saved only in the app sandbox for moments.
How do I change the language? Go to Me → Settings → Language and choose Simplified Chinese or English. The interface updates immediately.
What do Face ID and background blur do? Face ID unlocks the app on this device. Background blur hides private notes in the app switcher. Biometrics never leave iOS.
How do I save a moment? Add a moment from Home or the Love Calendar. Fill in a title, mood, optional place, and photo, then save. Favorite or copy quotes in the Library, and track date-night checklists in Plans.
How do I delete my data? Delete individual entries in the app, or use Privacy & Security → Clear Local Cache. Uninstalling also removes sandbox files. Email us if you want feedback you sent by mail removed.

常见问题

数据保存在哪里？ 日常、瞬间、照片和纪念日默认只保存在本机，不会上传到我们的服务器。卸载应用或清除缓存前，请确认你已做好设备备份。
收不到纪念日提醒？ 请到 iPhone「设置 → 通知 → Lovelit」开启允许通知，并在应用「提醒设置」中打开对应开关。本地提醒依赖系统通知权限。
无法添加照片？ 请在「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片」中允许 Lovelit 访问相册。照片仅写入应用沙盒，供瞬间展示使用。
如何切换中英文？ 打开「我的 → 设置 → 语言」，选择简体中文或 English。界面文案会立即切换。
Face ID 和后台模糊做什么？ Face ID 用于本机解锁应用；切到后台时模糊内容可避免锁屏或多任务界面露出私密记录。生物特征由 iOS 保管。
如何记录我们的瞬间？ 在首页或恋爱日历点击添加，填写标题、心情、可选地点和照片后保存。图书馆可收藏或复制浪漫文案；计划页可勾选浪漫场景清单。
如何删除数据？ 可在应用内删除单条记录，或使用「隐私与安全 → 清除本地缓存」。卸载应用也会移除沙盒中的内容。如需删除你曾邮件发给我们的反馈，请来信说明。
