Amour Privacy Policy

欢迎使用 Amour（中文名「恋爱指南」，以下简称「本应用」或「我们」）。我们重视您的隐私，并希望通过本政策说明我们如何收集、使用、存储与保护您的信息。

Welcome to Amour (known in Chinese as「恋爱指南」, hereinafter “the App” or “we”). We respect your privacy, and this policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your information.

1. 适用范围

1. Scope

本隐私政策适用于 Amour iOS 应用程序及其提供的相关功能与服务。使用本应用即表示您已阅读并理解本政策。

This Privacy Policy applies to the Amour iOS application and the features and services it provides. By using the App, you confirm that you have read and understood this policy.

2. 我们收集的信息

2. Information We Collect

本应用以本地存储为主，不会要求您注册账号。我们可能涉及以下信息：

The App is primarily local-storage based and does not require you to register an account. The following information may be involved:

  • 您主动填写的内容：情侣昵称、纪念日备注、甜蜜清单、心情日记、个人资料等。
  • 您选择的照片：当您设置情侣头像时，您从系统相册中选取的图片（仅在您授权后访问）。
  • 应用偏好设置：语言选择、通知开关等本地配置。
  • 设备信息（有限）：为维持应用正常运行，系统可能提供基础设备与系统版本信息；本应用当前版本不会主动上传此类信息至我们的服务器。
  • Content you enter: couple nicknames, anniversary notes, sweet checklists, mood journals, profile details and similar entries.
  • Photos you select: images you pick from your system photo library when setting a couple avatar (accessed only after you grant permission).
  • App preferences: local settings such as your language choice and notification toggles.
  • Limited device information: the system may supply basic device and OS version details so the App can run correctly. The current version does not upload such information to our servers.

3. 系统权限说明

3. System Permissions

  • 相册（读取）：用于选择情侣头像照片。仅在您主动操作时请求访问。
  • 相册（写入）：用于将恋爱成就海报保存至您的照片图库。仅在您点击保存时请求访问。
  • Photo library (read): used to choose a couple avatar. Access is requested only when you actively start the action.
  • Photo library (write): used to save achievement posters to your photo library. Access is requested only when you tap save.

您可以在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片」中随时管理或撤销授权。

You can manage or revoke these permissions at any time under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

4. 信息的使用方式

4. How We Use Information

我们收集的信息仅用于：

The information we collect is used solely to:

  • 在本设备上展示和管理您的恋爱记录与个性化内容；
  • 提供清单、日记、攻略、测验等功能体验；
  • 根据您的设置保存语言偏好与通知选项；
  • 在您明确操作时，保存分享海报至相册。
  • display and manage your relationship records and personalized content on this device;
  • power features such as checklists, journals, guides and quizzes;
  • remember your language preference and notification options;
  • save share posters to your photo library when you explicitly ask for it.

我们不会将您的恋爱记录用于广告定向，也不会出售您的个人信息。

We do not use your relationship records for ad targeting, and we do not sell your personal information.

5. 信息存储与安全

5. Storage and Security

  • 您的清单、日记、资料及头像文件默认保存在您的设备本地。
  • 当前版本不会将您的情侣记忆同步或上传至我们的云端服务器。
  • 卸载本应用或清除应用数据可能导致本地内容无法恢复，请自行做好备份（如保存重要照片或截图）。
  • Your checklists, journals, profile details and avatar files are stored locally on your device by default.
  • The current version does not sync or upload your shared memories to our cloud servers.
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing its data may make local content unrecoverable, so please keep your own backups (for example, save important photos or screenshots).

6. 第三方服务

6. Third-Party Services

当前版本不集成第三方广告 SDK，也不向第三方共享您的个人恋爱数据。若未来版本引入第三方分析或云服务，我们将更新本政策并在应用内告知。

The current version does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs and does not share your personal relationship data with third parties. If a future version introduces third-party analytics or cloud services, we will update this policy and notify you within the App.

7. 您的权利

7. Your Rights

您有权：

You have the right to:

  • 在应用内编辑或删除自定义清单项、日记条目及个人资料；
  • 重置情侣头像，恢复为默认演示头像；
  • 在系统设置中撤销相册等权限；
  • 通过卸载应用删除存储在本设备上的全部应用数据。
  • edit or delete custom checklist items, journal entries and profile details inside the App;
  • reset couple avatars back to the default sample images;
  • revoke photo library and other permissions in system settings;
  • remove all App data stored on this device by uninstalling the App.

8. 未成年人保护

8. Protection of Minors

本应用面向年满 13 周岁的用户。若您未满法定年龄，请在监护人同意与指导下使用。我们不会有意收集未成年人的个人信息。

The App is intended for users aged 13 and above. If you are under the age of majority, please use it with the consent and guidance of a guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

9. 政策更新

9. Policy Updates

我们可能适时修订本隐私政策。更新后的版本将注明「最后更新」日期。重大变更时，我们将在应用内或通过其他合理方式提醒您查阅。

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will carry a new “last updated” date. For significant changes, we will remind you to review the policy within the App or by other reasonable means.
