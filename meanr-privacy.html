欢迎使用 Meanr（以下简称「本应用」或「我们」）。Meanr 是一款基于设备端人工智能的照片风格化工具。

我们重视您的隐私。本隐私政策说明我们在您使用 Meanr iOS 应用时如何收集、使用、存储、共享和保护您的信息，以及您享有的相关权利。使用本应用即表示您同意本政策的条款。

我们收集的信息

根据功能需要，我们可能收集或处理以下类型的信息：

1. 照片数据

  • 您从系统照片选择器中选择的照片，用于 AI 风格化与滤镜处理。
  • 核心 AI 风格化功能在您的设备本地完成，照片不会为风格化处理上传至我们的服务器。
  • 生成的作品默认保存在设备本地，您可随时在应用内删除。

1A. 人脸数据（Face Data）

  • 当您选择包含人脸的照片并进行风格化时，本应用可能在设备本地使用 Apple Vision 框架临时分析人脸区域，包括人脸边界框及面部关键点/轮廓等几何信息，用于裁剪、对齐或生成局部蒙版，以便完成风格化效果。
  • 上述人脸几何数据仅在处理过程中临时保存在设备内存中，不会单独持久化为人脸模板、生物识别档案或人脸识别身份信息，也不会用于身份验证、用户识别或跨会话追踪。
  • 人脸数据不会上传至我们的服务器，也不会与第三方共享。处理完成后，相关临时几何数据会随内存释放而丢弃。
  • 您主动保存的风格化作品（图像本身）可能仍显示面部外观；该类作品默认仅存储于设备本地应用沙盒/相册（若您选择保存），您可在应用内删除作品，或通过卸载应用清除本地数据。

2. 设备与标识信息

  • 设备标识符（如 IDFV），用于应用功能、账户关联与服务稳定性。
  • 应用版本、系统版本、渠道号等基础设备信息。

3. 推送通知信息

  • 在您授权后，我们可能收集 Apple 推送通知服务（APNs）设备令牌，用于向您发送服务通知。

4. 广告归因与追踪信息

  • 在您授权「允许 App 请求跟踪」后，我们可能通过 AppsFlyer 等第三方服务收集广告标识符（IDFA）及归因数据，用于衡量广告效果与优化产品体验。
  • 若您拒绝授权，我们将不会通过 IDFA 进行跨 App 追踪，但仍可能使用不关联个人身份的聚合数据。

5. 应用偏好与本地缓存

  • 您的设置偏好、作品历史记录元数据、本地缓存等，存储在设备本地（如 UserDefaults、Keychain、应用沙盒目录）。

Introduction

Welcome to Meanr (the "App" or "we"). Meanr is an on-device AI photo stylization tool.

We value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you use the Meanr iOS app, and the rights you may have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect or process the following types of information:

1. Photos

  • Photos you select via the system photo picker for AI stylization and filters.
  • Core AI stylization runs locally on your device. Photos are not uploaded to our servers for AI processing.
  • Generated works are stored locally on your device by default and can be deleted at any time in the App.

1A. Face Data

  • When you select a photo that contains a face and run stylization, the App may temporarily analyze face regions on-device using Apple’s Vision framework. This may include face bounding boxes and facial landmark/contour geometry used only to crop, align, or create local masks so stylization can be applied correctly.
  • This face geometry is held only transiently in device memory during processing. It is not persisted as a face template, biometric profile, or facial-recognition identity record, and it is not used for authentication, user identification, or cross-session tracking.
  • Face data is not uploaded to our servers and is not shared with any third party. After processing finishes, the temporary geometry is discarded when memory is released.
  • Stylized images you choose to keep may still depict a face as visual content. Those images remain on-device in the app sandbox and/or your Photos library if you save them. You can delete works in the App or remove local data by deleting the App.

2. Device and Identifier Information

  • Device identifiers (such as IDFV) for app functionality, account association, and service stability.
  • Basic device information such as app version, OS version, and channel ID.

3. Push Notification Information

  • With your permission, we may collect Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device tokens to send you service-related notifications.

4. Advertising Attribution and Tracking

  • With your permission via App Tracking Transparency (ATT), we may collect advertising identifiers (IDFA) and attribution data through third-party services such as AppsFlyer to measure ad performance and improve the product experience.
  • If you deny tracking permission, we will not use IDFA for cross-app tracking, but we may still use aggregated data that does not identify you personally.

5. App Preferences and Local Cache

  • Your settings, work history metadata, and local cache are stored on your device (e.g., UserDefaults, Keychain, app sandbox).

How We Use Information

  • Provide core features such as photo stylization, filters, and work management;
  • Maintain app security, troubleshoot issues, and improve performance and user experience;
  • Send push notifications (only with your permission);
  • Perform advertising attribution analysis and product analytics;
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

On-Device Processing

Meanr's core AI models run on-device using Apple Core ML. Photos you select are processed locally for inference and rendering without uploading to the cloud for stylization. This helps reduce privacy risk and improve processing speed.

Some auxiliary features (such as configuration loading, web content display, and push registration) may involve network communication with servers or third-party services, but your original photos are not uploaded for AI inference.
