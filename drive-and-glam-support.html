FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. Drive & Glam works entirely on your device. No sign-in is required.

Where are my photos stored?
Uploaded photos are saved locally on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to any server or cloud service.

How do I upload photos?
Tap Upload → select from your library or camera → choose a category → tap Publish.

How do I save or share a photo?
Open any photo and use Save, Share, or Wallpaper from the detail screen.

How do I manage favorites?
Tap the heart icon on any photo. View all favorites from your profile.

How do I clear my data?
Go to Me → Settings and use Clear Cache or Clear My Uploads.

Which languages are supported?
English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. Change language in Settings.

Privacy

Drive & Glam does not collect personal data. All gallery content and uploads remain on your device. Read our Privacy Policy.
