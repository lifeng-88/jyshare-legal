User Agreement

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Using QiRi means you agree to this page. QiRi is an offline life notebook for iPhone. The interface is in Chinese. It requires iOS 15 or later and stays in portrait.

What QiRi does

You can keep daily habit check-ins, one mood, and journal notes. The first launch includes four sample habits: drink water, walk, read, and sleep early. You can rename them, change the symbol and color, reorder them, delete them, or add your own. A reminder can fire on the phone at a time you choose. The notebook, moods, check-ins, and the on-device reminder do not need a network. AppsFlyer uses the network to record where the install came from.

Your writing stays yours

Your name, habits, check-ins, moods, and journal entries belong to you. QiRi stores them on this iPhone so you can read them. The developer does not take ownership of that writing and does not use it for display, training, or sale.

You keep the backup

Before you change phones, delete the app, or clear records, export a backup from Profile. The backup is a JSON file. Without that file, the developer cannot restore anything after you delete QiRi or choose “清空这台手机上的记录”. Restoring a backup replaces the records currently on the phone.

Reminders

The daily reminder starts at 21:30 and you can change it. If iOS has not allowed notifications, it will not fire. Focus, Do Not Disturb, and the system notification settings still apply.

How to use it

  • Use QiRi as your own notebook
  • Export a backup before you store that file anywhere else
  • Do not use QiRi to interfere with other people, or turn it into a tool that damages someone else’s device

Provided as it is

QiRi is provided as the current version. It writes your records on the device, and it cannot promise to recover them if the phone is lost, the system fails, or no backup was exported. To the extent the law allows, the developer is not liable for records lost that way.
