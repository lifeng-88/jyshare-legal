1. 引言

欢迎使用 ChoreoAI（以下简称「本应用」）。我们重视您的隐私，并致力于保护您的个人信息。本政策说明我们如何收集、使用、存储与保护与本应用相关的信息。使用本应用即表示您已阅读并理解本政策。

2. 我们收集的信息

根据您使用的功能，我们可能处理以下信息：

  • 设备与系统信息：设备型号、操作系统版本、应用版本等，用于保障兼容性与基础服务稳定。
  • 相机画面（本地处理）：在「AI 练习 / 姿态追踪」功能中，本应用需访问相机以进行动作捕捉与姿态纠错。相机画面主要用于实时分析；除非您另行明确授权或后续版本另行告知，我们不会将原始摄像内容上传用于长期存储。
  • 偏好与训练相关本地数据：如昵称、语言、通知开关、触感反馈、镜像设置、打卡与日程状态、练习统计等，主要保存在您的设备本地（如系统 UserDefaults）。
  • 网络内容请求：加载课程封面、教学视频等在线资源时，会产生必要的网络访问记录（由您的网络服务商或内容分发方按各自政策处理）。
  • 您主动提交的内容：如通过帮助与反馈功能填写的意见（若后续接入邮件或服务器，将按提交渠道处理）。

3. 信息使用目的

  • 提供舞蹈教程浏览、日程安排、AI 姿态练习与纠错等核心功能；
  • 保存您的偏好设置与学习进度，改善使用体验；
  • 进行故障排查、安全防护与产品改进；
  • 在法律法规要求或经您同意的情况下用于其他合理用途。

4. 权限说明

  • 相机：用于 AI 动作捕捉与实时姿态纠错练习。您可在系统设置中随时关闭权限；关闭后相关功能将不可用。
  • 网络：用于加载课程媒体与在线内容。
  • 通知（可选）：用于课程或练习相关提醒（若您开启）。

5. 信息共享与披露

我们不会出售您的个人信息。仅在以下情形可能共享或披露必要信息：

  • 获得您的明确同意；
  • 为提供服务所必需的第三方基础设施（如云存储、内容分发），且其仅可在服务目的范围内处理；
  • 依据法律法规、诉讼、政府主管部门的强制性要求；
  • 在合并、收购、资产转让等情形下，依法向承继方转移并继续受本政策约束。

6. 存储与安全

我们采取合理的技术与管理措施保护信息安全。本地偏好数据主要存储于您的设备。请妥善保管设备，勿在越狱或不可信环境中使用本应用。请注意，任何互联网传输或电子存储方式都无法保证绝对安全。

7. 未成年人

本应用主要面向一般用户。若您为未成年人，请在监护人同意与指导下使用。我们不会故意收集儿童的个人信息；如发现不当收集，将尽快删除相关数据。

8. 您的权利

您可以在应用内重置打卡、清除日程、修改昵称与偏好设置。您也可通过系统设置管理相机、通知等权限，或卸载应用以停止使用。如需行使查阅、更正、删除等权利，请通过支持页面联系我们。

9. 第三方服务与链接

本应用可能展示或跳转至第三方内容（如视频资源）。第三方的隐私做法由其自行负责，建议您查阅其政策。

10. 政策更新

我们可能适时更新本政策。更新后将在本页面公布并调整生效日期。重大变更时，我们会通过应用内提示或其他合理方式通知您。

1. Introduction

Welcome to ChoreoAI (“the App”). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information related to the App. By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

  • Device and system information: device model, OS version, app version, and similar data used to maintain compatibility and service stability.
  • Camera frames (primarily on-device): for AI Practice / pose tracking, the App accesses the camera for motion capture and posture feedback. Frames are used for real-time analysis. Unless you provide additional consent or a future version states otherwise, we do not upload raw camera footage for long-term storage.
  • Preferences and training-related local data: nickname, language, notification toggles, haptics, mirror mode, check-in and schedule state, practice stats, and similar settings primarily stored on your device (e.g., UserDefaults).
  • Network content requests: loading course covers and instructional videos may generate necessary network logs handled by your carrier or content delivery providers under their own policies.
  • Content you submit: feedback you provide through Help (if routed via email or a server, it will be processed through that channel).

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide core features such as tutorial browsing, scheduling, and AI pose practice/feedback;
  • To save preferences and learning progress and improve experience;
  • For troubleshooting, security, and product improvement;
  • For other reasonable purposes required by law or with your consent.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: required for AI motion capture and real-time posture correction. You may revoke access in system settings; related features will then be unavailable.
  • Network: used to load course media and online content.
  • Notifications (optional): used for course or practice reminders if enabled.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share or disclose information only when:

  • You give clear consent;
  • It is necessary for infrastructure providers (e.g., CDN/cloud) strictly for delivering the service;
  • Required by law, legal process, or competent authorities;
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer, subject to continued protection consistent with this Policy.

6. Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. Local preference data is mainly stored on your device. Please keep your device secure and avoid using the App on jailbroken or untrusted environments. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Children

The App is intended for a general audience. If you are a minor, use the App only with guardian consent and guidance. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information; if we learn of improper collection, we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Choices

You may reset check-ins, clear schedules, and edit nickname/preferences in the App. You may manage camera and notification permissions in system settings, or uninstall the App to stop using it. To request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, contact us via the Support page.

9. Third-Party Services and Links

The App may display or link to third-party content (such as videos). Third parties are responsible for their own privacy practices.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. For material changes, we may notify you in-app or by other reasonable means.
