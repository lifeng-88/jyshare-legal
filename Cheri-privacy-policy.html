隐私政策

Chéri 是一款放在你自己 iPhone 上的手帐。这篇政策说明应用如何处理与你有关的信息。继续使用 Chéri，即表示你已阅读本政策。

我们是谁

Chéri（套装标识符 com.cheri.herlife）由本应用的开发者提供。我们没有单独的账号系统。若要联系我们，请写到 support@cheri.app。

保存在设备上的信息

你写下的内容和做出的选择，都留在这台设备上，用于显示和继续编辑手帐。其中包括：

  • 手帐标题、正文、日期、心情、标签、天气、摘录，以及你标记为私密的篇章。
  • 尚未保存的草稿。
  • 你拍摄或从相册选择、并插入手帐的照片，以及你写的图片说明。照片保存在应用自己的本地目录中。
  • 你为「留声」选择的名称和时长。这是一条备忘标签，应用不会打开麦克风录音。
  • 习惯完成情况、心情记录、灵感收藏与喜欢、主题，以及晨昏提醒是否打开。

这些内容写入设备上的应用存储。我们没有服务器来接收或保存你的手帐。

系统权限

  • 相机。仅在你选择拍摄拍立得时使用，用来把照片放进当前手帐。
  • 照片。仅在你选择从相册插入图片时使用。
  • 通知。仅在你打开晨昏提醒时申请。提醒由系统在本机定时发出，不是远程推送。你可以拒绝，应用的记事功能仍然可用。

可在 iPhone 的「设置」中随时关闭这些权限。

我们不会做的事

  • 不要求注册账号，也不收集姓名、电话或电子邮箱，除非你主动写信给我们。
  • 不把你的手帐、照片或心情上传到我们的服务器。
  • 不出售个人信息，不接入广告或跨应用追踪。
  • 不读取通讯录、位置或麦克风。

网络与第三方

浏览灵感页时，部分配图会从互联网加载。提供这些图片的服务器可能看到普通的连接信息，例如 IP 地址和请求时间。这些请求不包含你的手帐正文。

你使用系统分享导出手帐时，内容会交给你选定的应用或对象。之后如何处理，由该应用自己的政策决定。

如果你打开了 iPhone 或 iCloud 设备备份，Apple 可能会把本机应用数据包含进备份。那是 Apple 的备份，不是我们提供的云端手帐。

保存与删除

信息会一直留在设备上，直到你删除对应手帐，或卸载 Chéri。卸载会删除应用在这台设备上的本地数据。若设备备份里已经有一份副本，需要在 Apple 的备份中另行删除。

儿童

Chéri 不是面向 13 岁以下儿童的产品。我们不会明知地收集儿童的个人信息。因为内容只存在于设备本地，家长可以删除手帐或卸载应用。

政策更新

若这篇政策有实质变更，我们会更新本页顶部的生效日期。更新发布后你继续使用应用，即表示你了解更新后的政策。

联系我们

隐私问题请发送到 support@cheri.app。也可以先看 支持页面。

Privacy Policy

Chéri is a journal that lives on your own iPhone. This policy explains how the app handles information about you. By continuing to use Chéri, you acknowledge this policy.

Who we are

Chéri (bundle identifier com.cheri.herlife) is provided by the developer of this app. There is no separate account system. To reach us, write to support@cheri.app.

Information stored on your device

What you write and the choices you make stay on the device, so the journal can be shown and edited later. That includes:

  • Entry titles, text, dates, moods, tags, weather, quotations, and entries you mark as private.
  • Drafts that have not been saved yet.
  • Photos you take or choose from your library and place in an entry, plus any caption you write. Photos are stored in the app’s own local folder.
  • The name and duration you pick for a sound note. This is a label you choose. The app does not open the microphone or record audio.
  • Habit check-offs, mood history, saved and liked inspirations, theme, and whether morning or evening reminders are on.

This information is written to app storage on the device. We do not operate a server that receives or keeps your journal.

System permissions

  • Camera. Used only when you choose to take a photo for the current entry.
  • Photos. Used only when you choose an image from your library.
  • Notifications. Requested only if you turn on daily reminders. Those reminders are scheduled on the device. They are not remote push messages. You can decline, and journaling still works.

You can turn these permissions off at any time in iPhone Settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to register, and we do not collect your name, phone number, or email address unless you write to us.
  • We do not upload your journal, photos, or moods to our servers.
  • We do not sell personal information, and the app does not include advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • We do not read your contacts, location, or microphone.

Network and third parties

While you browse inspirations, some images are loaded from the internet. The servers that host those images may see ordinary connection details, such as an IP address and the time of the request. Those requests do not include your journal text.

When you export an entry with the system share sheet, the content goes to the app or person you choose. What happens next is covered by that app’s own policy.

If iPhone or iCloud device backup is on, Apple may include this app’s local data in the backup. That is Apple’s backup, not a journal cloud we provide.

Retention and deletion

Information remains on the device until you delete the entry or uninstall Chéri. Uninstalling removes the app’s local data from that device. A copy that is already in a device backup has to be removed from Apple’s backup separately.

Children

Chéri is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because content stays on the device, a parent can delete entries or uninstall the app.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the app after the update is posted means you have read the revised policy.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to support@cheri.app. You can also start from the support page.
