This Privacy Policy explains how NeonForm (“we”, “the App”) handles information when you use our iOS fitness application. NeonForm is designed to keep most of your workout data on your device.

1. Who we are

NeonForm is an AI-assisted form coaching and workout tracking app for iPhone (portrait mode). Bundle identifier: com.neonform.fitness.

2. Information we process

  • Workout records you generate in the App (duration, calories estimates, scores, completed exercises).
  • Preferences such as display name, daily/weekly goals, rest interval, voice coaching toggle, bookmarks, today’s plan, and language setting.
  • Optional device features you enable, such as spoken coaching cues via the system speech synthesizer.

We do not require an account, and we do not intentionally collect government IDs, payment card numbers, or precise continuous location for advertising.

3. Camera and motion analysis

NeonForm presents an on-device form-guidance experience. Depending on your device permissions and product version, camera or pose-related processing—if enabled—is used only to provide coaching feedback during a session. Imagery is processed for the live session experience and is not uploaded by the App for cloud model training unless a future version explicitly asks for your consent and discloses that use.

4. Where data is stored

Core history, bookmarks, plans, and profile data are stored locally in the App’s sandbox (Application Support). Language preference is also stored on device. Clearing local data in Settings wipes demo/user records stored by the App on that device.

5. Network and third parties

The current NeonForm build is intended to work primarily offline for training logs. We do not sell your personal information. If a future release introduces analytics, crash reporting, or cloud sync, we will update this Policy and request any required permissions/consent.

6. Children

NeonForm is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). Do not use the App if you are under the applicable age.

7. Your choices

  • Change language inside the App (Profile → Preferences).
  • Turn voice coaching on or off.
  • Delete individual history items or clear local data from Profile.
  • Revoke camera/microphone-related system permissions in iOS Settings.

8. Data retention

Local records remain until you delete them, clear App data, or remove the App. Uninstalling NeonForm removes its sandbox data on that device.

9. International users

The App may be used in multiple regions. Because processing is primarily on-device, cross-border transfers of workout content are not a default part of the current product.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page and, where appropriate, in-app notice.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@neonform.app
Support: see NeonForm Support.

NeonForm 提供设备端的姿态指导体验。根据系统权限与产品版本，若启用相机或姿态相关处理，仅用于会话中的纠错反馈。影像用于实时训练体验；除非未来版本明确征求同意并另行披露，本应用不会将其上传用于云端模型训练。

当前版本的训练记录功能以离线使用为主。我们不出售您的个人信息。若后续版本引入统计分析、崩溃上报或云同步，我们将更新本政策，并依法申请所需权限或征得同意。

NeonForm 不面向 13 岁以下（或您所在地区要求的最低年龄以下）的儿童。若未达到适用年龄，请勿使用本应用。

7. 您的选择

  • 在应用内切换语言（个人资料 → 训练偏好）。

本地记录将保留至您删除、清除应用数据或卸载应用为止。卸载 NeonForm 会移除该设备上的应用沙盒数据。
