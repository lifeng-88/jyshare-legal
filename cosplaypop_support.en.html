Support

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Contact Us

If you need help with CosplayPop, have feedback, or want to report a problem, contact our support team:

Email: support@cosplaypop.app

We aim to respond within 3–5 business days. Complex issues may take longer to investigate.

Before You Write

To help us resolve your issue faster, please include:

  • a clear description of the problem or question;
  • your device model and iOS version;
  • your CosplayPop app version (shown in Settings);
  • screenshots or screen recordings, if available; and
  • steps to reproduce the issue, if applicable.

Common Topics

  • Account and profile — editing display name, avatar, bio, or cover image.
  • Publishing content — uploading photos, adding captions or tags, and managing visibility.
  • Notifications — enabling or disabling push notifications in iOS Settings.
  • Photo library access — granting permission to upload or save media.
  • Language settings — changing display language in App Settings.
  • App performance — slow loading, crashes, or display issues.

Account and Content Requests

For privacy-related requests such as access, correction, or deletion of personal information, contact:

Email: privacy@cosplaypop.app

For legal or terms-related questions, contact:

Email: legal@cosplaypop.app

Feedback

We welcome suggestions to improve CosplayPop. Share your ideas at support@cosplaypop.app with the subject line “Feedback”.
