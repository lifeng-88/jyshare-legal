FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. You can use the App without registering.
How do I add a memory or classmate?
Tap “+” on the Gallery or Classmates tab, pick a photo, fill in the details, and save.
How do I edit or delete content?
Open the detail view, tap “⋯”, or long-press a card. You can edit or delete items you created. Built-in sample content cannot be deleted.
Why can't I save to the photo library?
Check iOS Settings → Privacy → Photos and allow AlumniRecord to add photos.
How do I change the language?
Go to Me → Language and choose English or Traditional Chinese.
Video won't play — what should I try?
Ensure iOS 15 or later, restart the App, and email support with your dev_id and a description if the issue persists.
